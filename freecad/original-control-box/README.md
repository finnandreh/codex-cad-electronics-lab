# Original parametric control box

The first enclosure in the series is a 140 × 90 × 50 mm rounded control box
with 3 mm walls and floor, a separate 3 mm lid, four lid screw positions,
internal mounting posts and a cable entry. The lid carries two 16 mm switch
holes and two 8 mm indicator holes.

The native FreeCAD document exposes driving dimensions in a spreadsheet. The
included case-only STEP and separate base/lid STL files are generated from the
same document.

Recorded software checks: valid base and lid solids, zero base/lid
intersection, two solids after STEP readback and closed STL meshes at Z=0.
Physical fit, screw choice, print tolerances, strength and electrical use have
not been tested.
