# Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W case

Reference: [official Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W drawing](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008304-DS).

The design starts from the official 51 × 21 mm board envelope. The rear 8 mm
of the board projects beyond the case material to keep material away from the
antenna edge. The generated case envelope is 51 × 37 × 18.65 mm.

Recorded software checks: valid base/lid solids, two-solid case STEP,
closed base/lid STL meshes and zero calculated base/lid/reference collision
volume. Vendor reference material is excluded from manufacturing exports.

The simplified reference does not establish component or connector heights.
Real-board fit, cable access, retention, print tolerances, thermal and RF
behaviour have not been physically tested.
