# Seeed XIAO SAMD21 v4 case

Reference: [official Seeed XIAO SAMD21 documentation](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Seeeduino-XIAO/).

The design uses the official v4 STEP reference. Its measured PCB bounds in the
recorded model are 21.30 × 17.90 × 1.61 mm, slightly different from the
nominal 21 × 17.8 mm product dimensions. The generated case envelope is
37.30 × 33.90 × 20.65 mm and targets this model revision, not XIAO Plus.

Recorded software checks: valid base/lid solids, case STEP readback, closed
STL meshes and zero calculated base/lid/reference collision volume. Vendor
geometry is excluded from the public manufacturing exports.

Real-board tolerances, underside pads, retention, cable access, printing, RF,
thermal behaviour and physical fit have not been tested.
